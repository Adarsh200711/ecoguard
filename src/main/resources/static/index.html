CRITICAL CONSERVATION AREA

Why Pallikaranai Marshland Needs Our Vigilance

Shrunk from over 5,000 hectares to approximately 600 hectares due to rapid urbanization, Pallikaranai is the only remaining natural freshwater wetland in Chennai.

115+ Avian Species

Breeding ground and refuge for migratory birds including Spot-billed Pelicans, Glossy Ibises, and Pheasant-tailed Jacanas threatened by non-biodegradable plastics.

Natural Flood Barrier

Acts as the primary flood sponge for southern Chennai, absorbing monsoon excess from 250 sq km. Debris dumping severely chokes natural drainage culverts.

Leachate & Fire Risks

Unregulated dumping causes hazardous chemical leachate that permeates the groundwater and triggers summer marshland fires destroying sensitive reed beds.

SYSTEM WORKFLOW

How EcoGuard Works

A streamlined 4-step pipeline designed in our project architecture to ensure every citizen report translates into accountable ground action.

1

Identify & Submit

Spot waste along Radial Road, Perumbakkam, or Velachery border and log category and location.

2

Backend Validation

Spring Boot REST service validates report data, allocates an incident ID, and records audit trail.

3

Action & Cleanup

Wetland patrol officers or volunteer squads review priority and execute cleanup operations.

4

Verified Closure

Status changes to 'Resolved' with comments; dashboard metrics update instantly.
