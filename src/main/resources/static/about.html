WETLAND CONSERVATION INITIATIVE

EcoGuard: Protecting Pallikaranai Marshland

EcoGuard is an integrated digital waste monitoring and incident reporting platform dedicated to safeguarding Chennai's last surviving natural freshwater wetland. By providing an open channel between active citizens, volunteer field cleanup crews, and conservation authorities, EcoGuard accelerates the identification, tracking, and accountable remediation of illegal dumping.

RAMSAR SITE #2484

The Ecological Jewel of South Chennai

Pallikaranai Marshland is one of the few natural wetland ecosystems remaining within the Chennai metropolitan limits.

Critical Hydrological Function

The marshland drains an immense catchment basin of over 250 square kilometers, collecting flood waters from south Chennai and discharging into the Bay of Bengal via Okkiyam Madavu. Unregulated construction debris and plastic damming choke these vital arteries.

Rich Biodiversity Haven

Home to 115+ bird species (including vulnerable species like Grey Pelican and Spot-billed Pelican), 10 species of mammals, 21 species of reptiles, 50 species of fish, and diverse aquatic flora essential for nitrogen filtering.

Pollution Pressures & Threats

Historically impacted by open dumping, medical waste, and raw sewage influx. Continued roadside garbage dumping and toxic runoffs threaten irreversible habitat fragmentation without coordinated digital surveillance.

EcoGuard System Architecture

Designed following 3-tier modular engineering principles for real-time wetland environmental surveillance.

1. Presentation Layer
  • Semantic HTML5 Structure
  • Vanilla CSS Design System
  • Async JavaScript Fetch API
  • 100% Mobile Responsive
2. Application Service Layer
  • Java 21 LTS Core
  • Spring Boot 4.x REST APIs
  • Jakarta Bean Validation
  • Status Audit History Logging
3. Database & Storage Layer
  • Spring Data JPA / Hibernate
  • H2 Embedded / MySQL RDBMS
  • Relational Schema with Foreign Keys
  • Parameterized Query Safety

What Citizens Can Do

  • Report any noticed plastic dumping or construction rubble immediately via EcoGuard.
  • Carry reusable fabric bags and stainless steel water bottles when passing through Radial Road.
  • Participate in volunteer weekend wetland cleanup and sapling plantation drives.
  • Spread awareness among roadside vendors against unsegregated food waste disposal.

Prohibited Activities

  • Dumping concrete, bricks, or excavation soil inside the wetland buffer zone.
  • Setting fire to roadside dry reed grass or plastic trash piles.
  • Discharging unverified liquid effluents or chemical runoff into marsh drains.
  • Littering single-use polythene packaging and alcohol bottles along water edges.
